# CuCP 90-Day Rollout Presentation Blueprint

This document replaces the risky Python executable `generate_presentation.py` with a secure, declarative markdown structure. This blueprint can be safely imported into presentation software or rendered securely.

## Slide 1: Title Slide
* **Title:** Turning the CuCP Framework into Adopted Reality
* **Subtitle:** A 90-day plan to implement and scale the Customer Collaboration Program.
* **Meta:** Role: Sr. Lead, P&T Operations | Scope: Days 1-90

## Slide 2: Change Management & Adoption Strategy
* **The Problem:** Fragmented Execution. Ad-hoc EAP/LA engagement, "Definition drift".
* **The Strategy:** Earn Adoption, Don't Mandate It. Lead with PO pain. Make first experience frictionless.
* **The Reframe:** "CuCP is your evidence kit — not a compliance checklist."

## Slide 3: Capability Classifications
* **Standard Capabilities:** Deterministic logic. Requires closed Beta and LA gating.
* **AI Capabilities:** Probabilistic models (LLMs). Gated via Open Beta with usage caps.
* **Proportional Governance:** Release gating must align to feature risk.

## Slide 4: 90-Day Rollout Execution
* **Phase 1 (Days 1-30):** Listen & Align. Shadow EAP releases, identify champions.
* **Phase 2 (Days 31-60):** Pilot & Refine. 2-3 live pilots. Act as Concierge.
* **Phase 3 (Days 61-90):** Scale & Institutionalise. All-hands enablement. Admin tasks automated.

## Slide 5: Enablement & Support Model
* **CuCP Concierge:** High-touch support during pilot.
* **Playbook + Cheat Sheets:** Self-serve starting Day 60.
* **Slack Support:** #cucp-support for real-time unblocking.
* **Champion-Led Rollout:** Peer credibility > authority.

## Slide 6: Measuring Initial Success
* **Leading Metric:** PO Satisfaction Score ("Is CuCP helping?")
* **Lagging Metrics:** % of eligible releases using CuCP, Customer participation rate.

## Slide 7: What Day 90 Looks Like
* **Evidence:** 50% eligible releases using CuCP, 2-3 champion PMs.
* **The Ask:** Exec sponsor for all-hands, Access to 2-3 willing pilot PMs.
